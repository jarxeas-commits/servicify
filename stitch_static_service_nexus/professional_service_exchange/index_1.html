Brand & Style

A trustworthy, modern marketplace for service providers and customers. The interface is clean, information-rich, and optimized for easy user flow across listings, booking, and messaging.

  • Professional, reliable, and empowering visual tone.
  • Corporate modern aesthetic focused on information clarity.
  • Generous whitespace, structured grids, and high-contrast elements.

Layout & Components

The system is built around a 12-column desktop grid, responsive stacking on mobile, and a consistent 16px spacing rhythm. Components are soft-rounded, elevated with subtle shadows, and designed for clarity.

Buttons

Primary, secondary, and ghost actions all use clear emphasis and consistent padding.

Cards

Cards feature 12px radius, subtle borders, and ambient shadows for visual hierarchy.

Inputs

Inputs are clean with a 1px border, 16px padding, and a color shift on focus.

Badges

Use badges for verified status, categories, and rating highlights.
